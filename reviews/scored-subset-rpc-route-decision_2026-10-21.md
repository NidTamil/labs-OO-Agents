## Decision needed

The held schema-two transport currently calls the pinned SunChaser alias as `root@...`; its candidate forced-command shim checks a root-owned Unix-socket pathname and then connects. No forced command is installed. A same-UID root process can replace that listener between check and connect. Another pathname check or `SO_PEERCRED` against UID 0 does not identify the genuine broker. The independent RPC0 review leaves this as P2. `claim` remains disabled, so there is currently no effect permission through this route.

Approve or reject a **schema-two-only** design change to a dedicated restricted SSH identity and manager-created socket-activated broker route. The account would execute only the fixed RPC shim, without an interactive shell, PTY, port forwarding or arbitrary command. Reviewed Unix permissions must let that account traverse and connect without unlinking, replacing or rebinding the listener or its parent directory. The current shim instead requires root-owned mode-0700 socket custody and assumes UID/GID 0; those checks, the broker's expected peer identity and their platform proof must change together. The trust boundary includes the privileged manager and broker: a privileged root actor is not excluded by account separation alone. Inherited-FD listener custody and protected directory proof are still required. The broker must inherit the manager's listener FD and remain verifier-only for `poll` and exact durable `ack`; a separate controller-side fresh-lease authority is required before any `claim`. Bind the listener registration to exactly one preparation and mailbox. The historical schema-one route and currently installed scored runtime must remain untouched during preparation.
